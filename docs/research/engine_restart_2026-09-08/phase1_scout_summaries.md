 - No fill model: 'trough < rung' (best bid dropping below a rung) is a proxy for a resting bid being hit; market-maker cancellations produce the same BBO signature without a fill, and there are zero live ladder fills to calibrate the mapping.

 - The full-grid regression coefficients (response_regression.csv) are uninterpretable because s and book log-odds are near-collinear and saturated; the nonparametric and contested-only tables carry the evidence.

RECS:
 - Treat the book as the outcome engine for elapsed 5-240 s: it is calibrated (slope 0.93-1.11) and already prices the strike lag at the open; do not spend design effort on open-time or mid-window mispricing under the 60 s rule.
 - Model the final minute as a two-state market: >90% of windows are decided (loser bid gone, winner at 0.99 behind a wall) and only ~8.5% are contested at k<=25. Any engine must first classify the window's state from the book (loser-bid presence, winner bid level), because the projection only adds value in the contested state.
 - Evaluate a taker-shaped candidate: in contested live books at k in [6,25), buy the projected side when the book P for that side is 0.5-0.9 and |s|<0.6; measured edge +6 to +14 pp over mid on 327-542 windows per 25 days. The design must subtract spread crossing, the 0.07*p*(1-p) fee and RTT, and be replayed against the CLOB prints (not the BBO) before any capital.
 - Add a reprice/pull classifier to the maker ladder's maintain(): complement ask +0.05 or own ask -0.05 within +-1 s marks a reprice (dumped side wins 50%, never recovers); a pure liquidity pull recovers in ~0.6 s and wins 71%. Cancel or skip rungs on reprices; hold on pulls.
 - Re-decide the 0.6 lock floor against the measured adverse selection: all 4 projection losses in the corpus (4/6,397 unconditional) occurred at 0.61<=s<0.84, k 15-23 s, and all 4 filled the ladder (4/34 dumped bot-zone windows); a floor of 1.0 removes them, a k cap of 15 removes them too (19/19 windows won at k in [6,15)). Size the trade-off on the 62 events, not on the unconditional lock accuracy.
 - Do not revive sign-agnostic dip buying: at k in [6,25) the 0.80 rung wins 72.2% (711 events), the 0.65 rung 61.8%, and at k>=25 every rung is 17-28 pp below break-even; the only positive dump slice is the projection side, consistent with r4.
 - Use the dead-book list (253 windows, dead_book_windows.csv) and a liveness check in every future replay: placeholder 0.50/0.51 books survive naive staleness filters and would manufacture fake edge in any contested-book study.
 - If the taker candidate is pursued, add the book-state fields this slice used (loser bid present, winner bid, reprice flag, contested indicator) to the decision path's recording so the fill-conditional win rates can be measured on real fills instead of the BBO proxy.

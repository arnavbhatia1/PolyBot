## 2.2 The ceiling

variant under the same fill physics beats this row. Two fill conventions:
*volume* = price-priority, volume-conserving (each sold share fills one rung —
the physical bound); *paper* = the deployed rule (each rung credits
independently — what the §2 paper bar would score).

through at least $500 (the four fills were multi-thousand-share dumps far
above our rung sizes).

## 2.3 The loser, characterized

Deep winner-token sells in the census samples are **concentrated, persistent,
and mostly not "panic"**:

- **A six-pseudonym cluster — seabears, pinkypanda, porkypie12, grumbong,
  wundawally, spork30 — appears in BOTH weeks** with identical geometry:
  sells at px 0.55–0.67, k ∈ [11,24] (q10–q90), ~40% of sampled deep share
  volume, each wallet ≈ break-even overall (they are flattening inventory
  into the close, not capitulating). Attribution: their deep sells are
  resting/crossing ASKS (maker-side), zero taker-sells — but a sell limit at
  0.61 crosses any higher resting bid first, so they remain real supply for
  rungs above their limit; the deployed print-through rule prices exactly this.
- The genuinely-diffuse taker-sell component is ~35–55% of deep volume,
  small anonymous wallets, names churn week to week.
- Concentration rose as supply fell: top-5 sellers held 48% of deep share
  volume in 08-21..27, **76% in 08-28..31**. Single-operator risk on the
  supply side is now material: if the cluster stops (as 0xAAAAA did),
  measured supply roughly halves again.
- Displacement: see §2.2 — the flow arrives at ~0 margin multiples.
  The cluster sells regardless of displacement (inventory clock, k≈20s),
  which is why it persists — and why the lock-gated ladder still can't have
  it: the lock almost never coincides with their selling moment.

## Bottom line

At $60 the market offers a $382/day oracle pie in our zone; the lock that
makes the strategy safe (and its record clean — 0 sign flips in 4,047 armed
windows, ANTI −$153k) structurally excludes ~98% of it. The honest ceiling of
the deployed system is **$5.6/day era-rate, ~$3/day at the current supply
regime**, reachable ~linearly up to several hundred dollars of budget. Fill
accrual toward the ≥20-window paper bar runs 0.22/day era-rate (~90 days),
slower at current supply. Live check: the epoch's first realized paper fill
landed 08-31 10:51Z (+$9.20, Up 32.5 sh at 0.72 blended, resolution win)
[data paper_0831.db pos 9622] — 1 fill / 3.9 epoch-days = 0.26/day, on the
replay's rate. Every lever that raises the rate (need 0.75/0.5, budget
fraction) is a frozen register entry with a scheduled ≥28-real-final-day
re-decision (~09-11).
